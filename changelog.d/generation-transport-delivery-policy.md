- Default native HTTP and direct Responses transport recovery to proven pre-send failures. A reset after submission or edge 5xx no longer silently replays a possibly executed request through these paths. `CODEX_ROUTER_NATIVE_RETRY_POLICY=availability` explicitly permits bounded ambiguous replay before client output and may repeat upstream work. Safe bounded attempt metadata records delivery evidence; client retries, provider retries, other adapters and intentional semantic repairs remain separate.
